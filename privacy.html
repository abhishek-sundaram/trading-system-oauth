Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Trading System Automation is a personal-use application for financial-market research and automated file management, not a public service.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the application uses the Google Drive drive.file scope to access files it creates or that the owner explicitly opens with the application. Such files may include financial research datasets.

Purpose and processing

Authorized files are uploaded, downloaded, organized and processed for the owner's research workflows. Processing may occur through owner-configured automation such as GitHub Actions.

Sharing and retention

The application does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers. Research files remain in the owner's configured storage until removed. Connected services may retain operational logs according to their own settings.

Security and user control

OAuth tokens are intended to be kept in private configuration or restricted secrets, not published on this website. The owner can revoke access through Google Account permissions and delete stored files.

Policy updates

This policy may change if the application's data practices change.
